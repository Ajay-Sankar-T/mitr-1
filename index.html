A Message to Our Community

At IIT Madras, we believe that academic excellence is deeply intertwined with emotional well-being. Our commitment extends beyond the classrooms and laboratories into the very hearts of our students.

Team MITR stands as a testament to our collective responsibility—to listen without judgment, to support without hesitation, and to ensure that no member of our community ever feels alone in their journey. Together, we are building a more empathetic and resilient campus.

Prof. Sathyanarayana N Gummadi (Dean of Students)
