Structure & Operations

Team MITR operates as a multi-tiered support system sanctioned by the Institute. Our structure ensures that every hostel, department, and social group has a direct link to wellness resources.

  • check_circle
    Peer Counselling System:

    Student coordinators are trained under medical professionals to ensure an appropriate approach to counselling.

  • check_circle
    Professional Oversight:

    All initiatives are guided by faculty advisors and professional psychologists from the Wellness Center.

All About MITR

MITR is guided by Our Dean of Student Prof. Sathyanarayana N Gummadi, Wellness Advisor Prof. Sarith P Sathian, Associate Dean (Wellness) Prof. Suresh Kumar Rayala and Wellness Officer Col. Balaji.

MITR team members are recruited every year in a multi layered manner. Applicants are screened via their applications and then sorted via interviews. To ensure our availability across the campus, A student from each department and Hostel is recruited and is given essential training.
