Structure & Operations

Team MITR operates as a multi-tiered support system sanctioned by the Institute. Our structure ensures that every hostel, department, and social group has a direct link to wellness resources.

  • check_circle
    Integrated Peer System:

    Student coordinators are embedded in every hostel for immediate, relatable support.

  • check_circle
    Professional Oversight:

    All initiatives are guided by faculty advisors and professional psychologists from the Wellness Center.

All About MITR

MITR is guided by our Dean of Student Prof. Sathyanarayana N Gummadi, Wellness Advisor Prof. Sarith P Sathian, Our Associate Dean (Wellness) Prof. Suresh Kumar Rayala and Wellness Officer Col. Balaji.

MITR team members are recruited every year in a very tedious manner. Applicants are screened and sorted via interviews. To ensure availability in each department, A student from each department and Hostel is recruited and are given barefoot training. The following is the Team’s vertical leads:
